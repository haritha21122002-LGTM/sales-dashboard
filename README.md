# 📊 Sales Dashboard – Power BI

An interactive **Sales Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profit, product quantity, payment methods, categories, cities, and yearly trends.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, DAX calculations, and data modeling.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This Power BI Sales Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance through multiple interactive visuals.

The dashboard helps analyze:

- Overall Sales Performance
- Total Profit
- Monthly Quantity Sold
- Product Performance
- Category-wise Profit
- Sales by Payment Method
- Year-wise Sales Distribution
- City-wise Data
- Product and Category Details
- Sales and Profit Margin

---

## 📊 Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. 📈 Month and Quantity Sold by Product

A monthly trend visualization showing:

- Monthly quantity sold
- Product quantity performance
- Monthly variations
- Comparison of quantity across different months

The dashboard provides monthly analysis from **January to December**.

---

### 2. 💳 Total Sales by Year and Payment Method

A year-wise payment method analysis covering:

- 2023
- 2024
- 2025

Payment methods include:

- Cash on Delivery
- Credit Card
- Debit Card
- UPI

This visualization helps compare the contribution of different payment methods across years.

---

### 3. 📦 Product, Category and City Details

The dashboard includes a detailed data table containing fields such as:

- Product ID
- Product Name
- Category
- City
- Customer-related information

This provides a detailed view of individual sales records.

---

### 4. 🏙️ City Selection

An interactive city-based selection area allows users to explore data based on different cities.

Examples visible in the dashboard include:

- Adoni
- Ahmedabad
- Agartala
- Ahmednagar
- Agra
- Aizawl

This makes the dashboard more interactive and allows users to explore geographical data.

---

### 5. 💰 Profit by Category

A treemap visualization displays profit generated by different product categories/products.

The visualization helps identify high-performing products based on profit contribution.

Examples shown include:

- Bottle
- Phone
- Paint
- Laptop
- Book
- Soap
- Shirt
- Bag
- Watch

---

### 6. 📊 Total Sales and Profit Margin by Category

This visualization provides a comparison between:

- Total Sales
- Profit Margin
- Category performance

It helps understand the relationship between sales and profitability across categories.
